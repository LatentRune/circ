Circ

A trade ledger for your guild, friends or realm. See who makes it, who buys it and who has it, so the gold stays with people you know.

For WoW Classic Era (1.15)

Download Circ How it works

Free and open source. Nothing to set up: it opens by itself the first time.

Trade with people you know

Every member's addon shares what they craft, what they pay for materials, what they want to buy and what they have to sell. Lookups work from day one: every Classic Era recipe comes with Circ.

See every part explained

Install

  1. Download the latest Circ-<version>.zip from Releases.
  2. Unzip it into World of Warcraft/_classic_era_/Interface/AddOns/, so you have AddOns/Circ/Circ.toc.
  3. Restart the game, or type /reload.

That's all

Circ opens by itself the first time, with a getting-started bar that shows one step at a time. You start in START, the ledger everyone with Circ shares. When you want your own ledger for a guild or friends, turn on Ledger management and invite them.
